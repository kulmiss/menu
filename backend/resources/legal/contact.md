## How to reach us

The fastest way is WhatsApp. You can also call or e-mail us. Our details are below and at the bottom of every page.

## When you write

Please tell us:

- your restaurant name or menu address,
- what you wanted to do,
- what happened (a screenshot helps).

Never send us your password. We will never ask for it.

## Restaurant owners

Signed in? Open **Help** in your admin. Most questions have a short answer there, with pictures.
