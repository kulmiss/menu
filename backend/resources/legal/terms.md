Menu is an online service that lets restaurants, cafés and other food businesses publish their menu and share it with guests through a link and QR codes. Menu is run by Kulmiss ("we", "us").

These Terms explain the rules for using Menu. By creating an account or using Menu, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use Menu.

## 1. Who these Terms are for

- **Restaurants:** the business that has a Menu account, and the people on its team (owners, managers and staff).
- **Guests:** people who open a restaurant's menu. Guests don't need an account. Sections 6, 7 and 9 to 13 apply to guests too.

## 2. Who can use Menu

- You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or join a team.
- Menu is for businesses. If you create an account for a restaurant, you confirm that you are allowed to act for that business.
- Give us true information when you sign up, and keep it up to date.

## 3. Your account

- Confirm your e-mail address after you sign up. If you don't confirm it within 7 days, we delete the new account and its restaurant.
- We may check new sign-ups before the menu goes live. If we refuse a sign-up, we tell you why.
- Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens in your account. We recommend turning on two-step sign-in in your profile.
- The owner decides who is on the team. Owners can invite managers and staff by e-mail and remove them at any time. Each role can only do what the app allows that role to do.
- If you think someone else is using your account, tell us at once at info@kulmiss.com.

## 4. Plans and payments

- **Free plan:** no cost and no end date, with unlimited dishes, menus and QR codes. Only photos and branches are counted, and some features are only in paid plans.
- **Paid plans** are for a fixed period: a month, or a year at a lower price. Current plans and prices are shown on our website or given to you by us.
- **How you pay:** we don't take card payments online. You pay us by bank transfer, mobile money, cash or another way we agree. When we receive your payment, we record it, add the paid time to your account and issue an invoice, which we send to the owners.
- **No automatic renewal:** paid plans don't renew on their own. Pay again before the end date to keep your plan.
- **Trials:** if we give you a trial, it ends on the date shown in your account.
- **When paid time or a trial ends:**
  - for 7 days, everything keeps working and you see a reminder;
  - for the next 14 days, your menu stays online, but you can't make changes;
  - after that, guests can't see your menu until you pay again. Your data is kept.
- **Smaller plan:** if you move to a smaller plan, nothing is deleted. You keep what you have, but you can't add more than the new plan allows, and features that aren't in the new plan are switched off.
- **Price changes:** we may change our prices. A change never affects time you have already paid for, and we tell owners before a new price applies to them.
- **Refunds:** we don't refund paid time you didn't use, unless we agree otherwise or the law requires it.

- You confirm that you have the right to use everything you upload, including photos and logos.

## 6. Prices, allergens and menu information

- The restaurant is responsible for its menu: prices, sizes, special and happy-hour prices, what is available, allergens, dietary labels, calories and descriptions.
- Restaurants: keep your menu correct and up to date. Guests rely on it.
- Menu shows what the restaurant enters. We don't check it, and we are not responsible for mistakes in it.
- Guests: if you have an allergy or a dietary need, always ask the restaurant's staff before you order.
- "My order" is a list on the guest's own phone, to show the waiter. It is not an order or a payment. The restaurant's bill is what counts.
