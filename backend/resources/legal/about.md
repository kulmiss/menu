## What we do

We make digital menus for restaurants, cafés, bars and juice bars. Guests scan a QR code at the table and see the menu on their phone. They don't need an app.

Restaurants change dishes, prices and photos themselves, from a phone or a computer. A change shows on the menu at once.

## What matters to us

- **Fast menus:** a menu opens in about a second, also on a slow phone and a weak connection.
- **Easy for everyone:** simple words, big buttons, and help on every page.
- **Privacy:** menu pages show no ads and use no trackers. Guests don't need an account.
- **Made for our region:** Amharic, Somali, Arabic and other scripts, local prices and right-to-left menus.
