Style **H · Arabic** has a warm sand page, your colour as a band at the top with a scalloped edge, gold ornaments between the categories and Kufi headings. Arabic, English and mixed dish names all look right.

![Style H · Arabic](shot:style-arabic)

**Menu direction**: under the style, choose how the menu runs:

- **Automatic**: right-to-left when most of your restaurant name, categories and dishes are in Arabic letters. Otherwise left-to-right.
- **Left to right**: for menus mostly in English.
- **Right to left**: the categories, prices, dish details<!-- if order_list -->, "+" buttons and **My order**<!-- endif --> all move to the other side.

The guest buttons (like "Search the menu") stay in English.

- **Dishes with a photo** show the photo in an arch. Without photos the dish name and price fill the card.
- **Your colour** is the band at the top, the prices and the buttons. Deep greens, blues and reds look best.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → H · Arabic**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
