Every table gets its own QR code. Then you can see which tables scan most in Insights.

1. Open **Branches**. Tap **Tables** on the branch.
2. Under **Add numbered tables**, type a name (for example "Table") and From 1 To 12. Tap **Add tables**.
3. Or add one table, for example "Terrace 2".
4. Tap **Print table cards**. You get four cards per A4 page, with cut lines.

Deleting a table makes its printed QR code stop working.
