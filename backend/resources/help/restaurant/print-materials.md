1. Open **QR codes → Print materials**.
2. Choose **What to print**: Table tent, Poster A5, Poster A4, Window sign, Round or Square stickers.
3. Choose a **Design** (Classic, Bold, Minimal) and **Colours** (Light or Dark).
4. Type a **Headline** if you like (up to 40 letters).
5. Choose **Which QR codes**. You get one page, tent or sticker for each code.
6. Tap **Download PDF**.

Going to a print shop? Turn on **For a print shop (3 mm bleed)**.

**For social media:** download a **Square post** or a **Story** picture with your code.
