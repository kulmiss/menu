A hidden dish stays saved, with its photos and prices. Guests don't see it.

1. Open the dish.
2. Turn off **Visible to guests**. Tap **Save**.

To show it again, turn **Visible to guests** on.

**Many dishes:** open **All dishes**, tick them and tap **Hide** or **Show**.

**In the app:** tap **Hide or show a dish** on Home.
