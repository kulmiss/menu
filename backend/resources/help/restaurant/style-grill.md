Style **N · Grill & BBQ** is always dark: charcoal, an ember glow and flames over grill bars at the top. Headings are tall and bold, and each price sits on a tag in your colour. Each category starts with a small icon, like a flame or a skewer.

![Style N · Grill & BBQ](shot:style-grill)

- **Category icons** follow the category name: "Tibs", "ጥብስ", "Hilib" or "Steak" get a flame, "Kebab" or "Nyama choma" a skewer. A name we don't know gets the next icon of the style.
- **Your own pictures:** a **banner** (Appearance) replaces the flames at the top. A **category photo** (Menu editor → the category → Photo) replaces that category's icon.
- **Turn the pictures off:** under the style, switch off **Show the style's pictures and icons**.
- It looks the same on light and dark phones, like F · Night Lounge.
- **Your colour** is used for the price tags and the buttons. Pick a warm, bright colour (orange, red, gold) for the best look.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → N · Grill & BBQ**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
