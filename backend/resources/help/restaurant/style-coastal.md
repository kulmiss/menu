Style **P · Coastal** has a deep-sea band at the top with a dhow under sail, the sun and a wave at its edge. The page is sand-coloured and the dish cards look like tickets with a coral edge. Each category starts with a small icon, like a fish or a prawn.

![Style P · Coastal](shot:style-coastal)

- **Category icons** follow the category name: "Fish", "Kalluun" or "سمك" get a fish, "Prawns" or "Lobster" a prawn, "Bariis" or "Rice" a rice bowl. A name we don't know gets the next icon of the style.
- **Your own pictures:** a **banner** (Appearance) replaces the dhow at the top. A **category photo** (Menu editor → the category → Photo) replaces that category's icon.
- **Turn the pictures off:** under the style, switch off **Show the style's pictures and icons**.
- It works right-to-left too (**Menu direction**), for Arabic menus.
- **Your colour** is used for the prices and the buttons. Coral, red and orange look best.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → P · Coastal**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
