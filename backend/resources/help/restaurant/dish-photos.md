Each dish can have up to **{{photos_per_dish}} photos**. The first one is the main photo. Guests swipe to see the others.

1. Open the dish.
2. Tap **Add photo**. On a phone you can tap **Take photo**.
3. Drag the photo to move it. Use the slider to zoom.
4. Tap **Use photo**.

To change the main photo, tap **Main** on another photo.

**Good photos**

- Use daylight from a window. Don't use the flash.
- Take the photo from above or from the side of the plate.
- Fill the picture with the food.
- A file can be up to {{photo_max_mb}} MB. JPEG, PNG and WebP work.

**iPhone:** HEIC photos can't be used. On the iPhone open **Settings → Camera → Formats** and choose **Most Compatible**.

Your plan allows {{signup_plan.photos}} photos in total on the {{signup_plan}} plan. See [plans](help:plans-compared).
