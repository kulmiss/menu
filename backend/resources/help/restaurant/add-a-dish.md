1. Open **Menu** and the category.
2. Tap **Add item**.
3. Type the **Name**, for example "Macchiato".
4. Type the **Price**, for example 120 or 120.50.
5. Add a **Short line**. Guests see it under the name.
6. Add a **Photo** (see [Dish photos](help:dish-photos)).
7. Tap **Save**.

![The dish editor](shot:dish-editor)

More tabs in the dish editor:

- **Prices & sizes**: for example Small and Large, each with its own price.
- **Add-ons**: choices like extra cheese. Guests see them as information.
- **Tags & allergens**: Vegan, Spicy, Halal, and allergens like milk or gluten.
- **New** and **Popular** badges. "New" can end by itself on a date.

**In the app:** tap **Add dish with photo** on Home. Take the photo, type the name and price, and tap **Save**.
