A sold-out dish stays on the menu with the label **Sold out**. Guests know it exists, but can't have it now.

**Mark a dish sold out**

1. Open **Menu editor** or **All dishes** (in the app: **Menu**).
2. Turn on the **Sold out** switch next to the dish.

**In the app:** tap **Mark sold out** on Home and tap the dishes.

**When it is back:** on the dashboard, find **Sold out now** and tap **Available again**.

![The sold-out list in the app](appshot:app-sold-out)

Staff can mark dishes sold out too.
