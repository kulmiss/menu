1. Open **Appearance**.
2. Tap **Brand colour** and pick your colour.
3. Choose a **Font**: Style default, Classic serif or Rounded.
4. Tap **Save**.

If your colour is hard to read on white, guests see a darker shade of it. Then the text stays readable.

Guests whose phone is in dark mode see a dark menu by themselves.
