Style **O · Garden Fresh** has a pale green top with a leafy branch and a sun. The cards are white with round corners, the colours are soft greens. Each category starts with a small icon in a green circle.

![Style O · Garden Fresh](shot:style-garden)

- **Category icons** follow the category name: "Juices" gets a glass, "Salads" a bowl, "Fasting", "ፆም" or "Vegan" a leaf. A name we don't know gets the next icon of the style.
- **Your own pictures:** a **banner** (Appearance) replaces the branch at the top. A **category photo** (Menu editor → the category → Photo) replaces that category's icon.
- **Turn the pictures off:** under the style, switch off **Show the style's pictures and icons**.
- Good for fasting menus: tag the dishes **Vegan** or **Fasting** so guests can filter them.
- **Your colour** is used for the prices and the buttons.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → O · Garden Fresh**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
