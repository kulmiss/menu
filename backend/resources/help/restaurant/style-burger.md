Style **R · Burger & Pizza** is bold and fun: a mustard band with a red-and-white checked edge and a burger and pizza slice drawn at the top. Dishes are big photo cards, two per row, with an ink outline, and each price sits on a tilted mustard sticker. Each category starts with a small icon, like a burger, a pizza slice or fries.

![Style R · Burger & Pizza](shot:style-burger)

- **Category icons** follow the category name: "Burgers" gets a burger, "Pizza" a pizza slice, "Chicken" or "Wings" a drumstick, "Fries" or "Sides" fries. A name we don't know gets the next icon of the style.
- **Your own pictures:** a **banner** (Appearance) replaces the drawing at the top. A **category photo** (Menu editor → the category → Photo) replaces that category's icon.
- **Turn the pictures off:** under the style, switch off **Show the style's pictures and icons**.
- It follows the guest's phone: light by day, dark at night.
- **Your colour** is used for the buttons and the chosen category. Pick a strong colour (red, orange, black) for the best look.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → R · Burger & Pizza**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
