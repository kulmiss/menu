Style **S · Injera & Tibs** looks like a white netela with a woven tibeb border. At the top, a charcoal band shows injera on the mitad and a clay pot of tibs, drawn in gold. Dish photos are round, like injera on its plate, with a thin gold ring, and a small tibeb stripe sits before each price.

![Style S · Injera & Tibs](shot:style-injera)

- **Category icons** follow the category name: "Injera", "Beyaynetu" or "እንጀራ" get an injera, "Wot", "Shiro" or "ፆም" a clay pot, "Tibs" a flame. A name we don't know gets the next icon of the style.
- **Your own pictures:** a **banner** (Appearance) replaces the drawing at the top. A **category photo** (Menu editor → the category → Photo) replaces that category's icon.
- **Turn the pictures off:** under the style, switch off **Show the style's pictures and icons**.
- It follows the guest's phone: light by day, dark at night.
- **Your colour** is used for the buttons and the chosen category. Pick a warm, deep colour (berbere red, coffee brown, deep green) for the best look.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → S · Injera & Tibs**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
