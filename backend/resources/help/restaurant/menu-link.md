Your menu is at **{{menu_url}}**.

- On the dashboard, tap **View menu** to open it.
- In the app, tap **Share menu link / QR** on Home. Choose WhatsApp, Telegram or any app.
- Put the link in your Instagram bio and your Google Maps listing.
