The **Finish setting up** list on your dashboard shows what is still missing. Each step opens the right page.

1. **Add your logo.** Go to **Appearance → Logo**. See [Logo and banner](help:logo-and-banner).
2. **Add a banner photo.** The wide photo at the top of your menu.
3. **Add at least 5 dishes.** See [Add a dish](help:add-a-dish).
4. **Add dish photos.** Dishes with photos get opened more.
5. **Set opening hours.** Go to **Restaurant info → Opening hours**.
6. **Add address and contact.** Also on **Restaurant info**.
7. **Download your QR codes.** See [QR codes](help:qr-codes).

When all steps are done, the list says 100 %. Tap **Hide this list** to remove it.
