---
title: My photos look blurry
topic: problems
summary: How to get sharp dish photos.
order: 20
keywords: blurry, pixelated, low quality, dark photo
---

- Use a photo of at least 1000 pixels wide. Photos from WhatsApp are often made small: send them as a **document**, or use the original from the camera.
- Clean the camera lens.
- Use daylight. Don't zoom in with the camera: move closer.
- Crop less. When you crop a small part, it gets blurry.

The menu makes small versions for fast loading. A sharp original stays sharp.
