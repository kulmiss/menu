Style **Q · Fine Dining** is calm and elegant: an ivory page with soft marble lines at the top, thin gold lines and a small diamond under your name. Headings use a refined serif and are centred. In dark mode it turns black and gold. Each category has a small gold icon.

![Style Q · Fine Dining](shot:style-fine-dining)

- **Category icons** follow the category name: "Wine" gets a glass of wine, "Main courses" a cloche, "Chef's specials" a star. A name we don't know gets the next icon of the style.
- **Your own pictures:** a **banner** (Appearance) replaces the marble at the top. A **category photo** (Menu editor → the category → Photo) replaces that category's icon.
- **Turn the pictures off:** under the style, switch off **Show the style's pictures and icons**.
- **Dishes with a photo** show it in a thin gold frame.
- **Your colour** is used for the prices and the buttons. Deep red, green or navy look best.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → Q · Fine Dining**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
