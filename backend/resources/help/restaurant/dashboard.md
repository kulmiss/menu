The dashboard shows **today** at your restaurant.

- **Menu views today**: how many times your menu was opened. Under it: yesterday's number.
- **QR scans today**: views that came from one of your QR codes.
<!-- if sold_out -->
- **Sold out now**: dishes guests see as sold out. Tap **Available again** when they are back.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if insights -->
- **Views and scans**: a small chart of the last 7 days. Tap **Insights** for more.
- **Dishes without a photo**: the most viewed dishes without a photo come first.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if order_list -->
- **"My order" lists**: how many lists guests made on their phones. See [My order](help:my-order).
<!-- endif -->
- **Plan and usage**: your plan, the days left and how much you use.

![The dashboard on a phone and a computer](shot:dashboard)

The numbers update within a minute. Search engines and link previews are not counted.
