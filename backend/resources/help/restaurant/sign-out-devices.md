**Other browsers:** open **Settings → Security → Other browsers**. Tap **Sign out of all other browsers**. This browser stays signed in.

**Phones with the app:** in the app, open **More → Profile → Devices**. Tap **Sign out this device** next to the phone.

Changing your password also signs out your other phones.
