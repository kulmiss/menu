Your menu has three levels:

- **Menus**, for example "All day" or "Drinks".
- **Categories** inside a menu, for example "Coffee" or "Breakfast".
- **Dishes** inside a category.

**Add a menu**

1. Open **Menu editor**.
2. Tap **New menu**. Type a name. Tap **Save**.

**Add a category**

1. Open the menu.
2. Tap **Add category**. Type a name, for example "Coffee". Tap **Save**.

**Change the order.** Drag a category or a dish by its handle. Guests see the new order at once.

**Show a menu only at some times.** Open the menu, tap **Edit menu**, then **When it is shown → At set times**. Add a time window, for example Monday 07:00 to 11:00. Times are in your restaurant's time zone.
