Open **QR codes**. You see a code for your restaurant<!-- if branches -->, each branch<!-- endif --> and each table.

1. Under **Download**, tap **PDF**, **PNG** or **SVG** next to a code.
2. Print it at 100 % size, at least 3 cm wide.
3. **Scan one test copy** with your phone before you print many.

**Extra codes** are for a flyer, a poster or the window. They are counted on their own in Insights.

1. Under **Extra code**, type a name, for example "Window poster".
2. Choose what it **Opens**. Tap **Add code**.

A printed code keeps working, even if you change your menu address. Deleting a code makes its copies stop working.
