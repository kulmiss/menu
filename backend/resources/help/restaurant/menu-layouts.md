Every menu style comes in three layouts. Pick one in **Appearance → 2. Layout**:

- **Grid**: big photos, two dishes per row (three on a computer).
- **List**: one dish per row, a small photo at the side.
- **Text**: no dish photos. Fastest to open. Your photos are kept for when you switch back.

Each style has its own layout, marked **This style's own**. B · Fresh grid, I · Islamic, R · Burger & Pizza and T · Juice & Drinks are grids; D · Minimal is text; the others are lists. You can always use the style's own layout and Text.
<!-- if !layouts -->
Using the other layout (a grid for a list style, or a list for a grid style) is in the Plus plan and up. See [plans](help:plans-compared).
<!-- endif -->

**See it before you save:** the phone on the right shows your menu as you change it. Tap **Light** or **Dark** above it to see how it looks on a phone in day mode or night mode. **Preview on a computer** shows it on a big screen.

Tap **Save**. Guests see the new layout at once.

**Try them first:** on the website's demo menus, use **Grid · List · Text** in the bar at the top (on a tablet or computer) to see any style in each layout.
