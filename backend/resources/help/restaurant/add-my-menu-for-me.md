No time to type all your dishes? We can do it for you.

1. Open **Dashboard** and find **Add my menu for me**.
2. Tap **Send photos of my menu** and choose the photos (one per page) or a PDF. Up to 10 files.
3. Write anything we should know, for example "the drinks are on the back". Tap **Send**.

You see "We got your menu" on the Dashboard. When we start, it says "We are typing in your menu now". When it is ready you get an e-mail, and you can change anything yourself.

- **Good photos:** flat, in good light, the whole page, not blurry. Prices must be readable.
- **iPhone photos (HEIC)** can't be read. In **Settings → Camera → Formats** choose **Most Compatible**, or take a screenshot of the photo.
- Your photos are kept privately and only the platform team sees them. They are deleted 30 days after your menu is done.
- You can have up to 3 menus waiting at the same time.

Prefer to do it yourself? See [Add a dish](help:add-a-dish).
