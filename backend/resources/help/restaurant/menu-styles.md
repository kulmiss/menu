Open **Appearance → Menu style**. The preview shows the change at once. Guests see it after you tap **Save**.

<!-- if style_editorial -->
- **A · Warm editorial**: big photos first and serif headings, like a printed café menu.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_grid -->
- **B · Fresh grid**: your name on the banner and two photo cards per row, like a food app.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_bistro -->
- **C · Quiet bistro**: a calm list with small photos on the right. Quickest to scan.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_minimal -->
- **D · Minimal**: text only. Names, short descriptions and prices. No dish photos. Fastest.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_mesob -->
- **E · Mesob**: warm colours, woven bands and round photos. Made for Ethiopian and Somali food. [More about E](help:style-mesob)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_lounge -->
- **F · Night Lounge**: always dark, with a soft glow in your colour. For bars and late nights. [More about F](help:style-night-lounge)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_board -->
- **G · Street Board**: a clean chalkboard with price tags. For fast food and juice bars. [More about G](help:style-street-board)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_arabic -->
- **H · Arabic**: Kufi headings, gold ornaments and your colour as a band. It can show the menu right-to-left. For Arabic, Yemeni and Middle Eastern food. [More about H](help:style-arabic)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_islamic -->
- **I · Islamic**: star patterns, an arch at the top and arched photos. Optional Halal badge and Ramadan mode. For halal and family restaurants. [More about I](help:style-islamic)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_somali -->
- **J · Somali**: ocean blues, woven-basket bands and waves. For Somali restaurants and cafés. [More about J](help:style-somali)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_mono -->
- **K · Black & White**: only black, white and greys, big type and fine lines. For modern cafés and fine dining. [More about K](help:style-black-white)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_colour -->
- **L · Colourful**: a bright colour for each category and rounded cards. For juice bars, ice cream and family places. [More about L](help:style-colourful)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_coffee -->
- **M · Coffee House**: a dark-roast band with a jebena and cups, round photos. For coffee houses and cafés. [More about M](help:style-coffee)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_grill -->
- **N · Grill & BBQ**: always dark, with embers, flames and price tags. For tibs, grill and burger places. [More about N](help:style-grill)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_garden -->
- **O · Garden Fresh**: leaves, soft greens and white cards. For juice houses, salad bars and fasting menus. [More about O](help:style-garden)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_coastal -->
- **P · Coastal**: a deep-sea band with a dhow, sand and coral. For fish and seafood places. [More about P](help:style-coastal)
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_luxe -->
- **Q · Fine Dining**: marble, thin gold lines and an elegant serif. For hotels and fine dining. [More about Q](help:style-fine-dining)
<!-- endif -->

Styles M to Q come with their own pictures: a drawing at the top and an icon for each category. Your banner and your category photos replace them, and **Show the style's pictures and icons** turns them off.

A style with a lock is not in your plan. See [plans](help:plans-compared).

![Appearance with the preview](shot:appearance)

**Themes** are ready-made looks. Pick one, then change the colour or font.
