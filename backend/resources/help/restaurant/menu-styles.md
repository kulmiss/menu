Open **Appearance → Menu style**. The preview shows the change at once. Guests see it after you tap **Save**.

<!-- if style_editorial -->
- **A · Warm editorial**: big photos first and serif headings, like a printed café menu.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_grid -->
- **B · Fresh grid**: your name on the banner and two photo cards per row, like a food app.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_bistro -->
- **C · Quiet bistro**: a calm list with small photos on the right. Quickest to scan.
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if style_minimal -->
- **D · Minimal**: text only. Names, short descriptions and prices. No dish photos. Fastest.
<!-- endif -->

A style with a lock is not in your plan. See [plans](help:plans-compared).

![Appearance with the preview](shot:appearance)

**Themes** are ready-made looks. Pick one, then change the colour or font.
