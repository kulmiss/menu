"My order" lets guests tap **+** on a dish to note it on their own phone, then show the list to the waiter. Nothing is sent to you and nothing is paid.

**To turn it off or on:** open **Appearance**, find **Show "Add to my order" (+) buttons on my menu**, switch it, and tap **Save**. The preview shows the change at once; guests see it as soon as you save.

When it is off, your menu has no **+** buttons, no list and no "Show to waiter". Dishes, photos, prices, sizes and add-ons still show as before, and the layout stays neat in every style. A list a guest saved earlier is removed from their phone.

If the switch is grey:

- **"Turned off by the platform"**: the platform has My order off for everyone (or for your restaurant). Ask us if you need it.
- **"Not in your plan"**: your plan doesn't include it. See [plans](help:plans-compared).

New restaurants start with My order **on**.
