- **Search:** type a few words, for example "price" or "QR".
- **The ? button** at the top of every page opens the article for that page.
- **Was this helpful?** Tap Yes or No under an article. It tells us which articles to make better.
- In the app, Help works **without internet** too. It shows the articles you loaded last.
