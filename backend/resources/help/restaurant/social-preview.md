When you share your menu link, apps show a small card: a picture, your name and a short text.

- **The picture** is your banner. No banner? Then a default picture is used.
- **The name** is your restaurant name (and the branch).
- **The text** is the short description from **Restaurant info**.

Changed your banner, but WhatsApp still shows the old picture? Apps keep their own copy for a while. Share the link again later, or in a new chat.
