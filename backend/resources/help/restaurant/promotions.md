A promotion is shown at the top of your menu. It can start and end by itself.

1. Open **Promotions**. Tap **New promotion**.
2. Choose the **Type**: **Special** (a dish) or **Announcement** (news, for example "Live music on Friday").
3. Type the **Title** and the **Text**. Add a **Photo** if you like.
4. For a special, choose the **Linked dish**. Guests can tap it to see the dish.
5. Set **Starts** and **Ends**. Leave Ends empty for no end.
<!-- if branches -->
6. Choose the **Branch**, or All branches.
<!-- endif -->
7. Tap **Save**.

Use the arrows to change the order. Turn off **Show on the menu** to pause a promotion.
