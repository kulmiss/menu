---

- **Wrong password?** Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. We send a link to your e-mail. Look in spam too.
- **"Too many tries"?** Wait one minute and try again.
- **No 2FA code?** Use one of your recovery codes instead.
- **Lost your phone and codes?** Contact support (below).
- **E-mail not confirmed?** Open the link from our first e-mail, or tap **Resend email**.
- **"Your account is not part of any restaurant"?** Ask the owner to invite you again.
