---
title: A dish doesn't show on my menu
topic: problems
summary: Check these things.
order: 30
keywords: missing dish, not showing, invisible, hidden
---

1. **Hidden?** Open the dish. Is **Visible to guests** on?
2. **Hidden category?** Open the category. Is it visible?
3. **Menu at set times?** A menu with **At set times** shows only in its time windows.
<!-- if branches -->
4. **Other branch?** Check **Branches → Prices & availability**. The dish may be hidden at that branch. Also check which menus the branch shows.
<!-- endif -->
5. **Old page?** Pull down on the phone, or close and open the menu again.
