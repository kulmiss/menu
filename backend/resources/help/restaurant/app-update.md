title: "Update required" and other app messages
topic: app
summary: What to do when the app asks you to update.
order: 40
keywords: update, new version, maintenance, not available
public: true
---

- **"Update required"**: your app is too old. Tap **Update now**, or open the Play Store or App Store and update **Menu Admin**.
- **"A new version of the app is ready"**: an update is ready. Tap **Update now**, or close the line and update later.
- **"Down for maintenance"**: we are updating the system. Wait a few minutes and tap **Try again**.
- **"The app is not available"**: use the website for now. Tap **Open the website**.

You can always use the website at {{admin_url}}.
