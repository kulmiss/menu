Style **K · Black & White** uses only black, white and greys. It has big serif headings, numbered categories (01, 02 …), fine lines and dotted lines from each dish to its price. It is white on phones in light mode and black on phones in dark mode.

![Style K · Black & White](shot:style-mono)

Under the style you find two options. Both are off until you turn them on:

- **Black-and-white photos**: shows your dish photos and banner in black and white. Your photos stay in colour in your account.
- **Use your brand colour as a small accent**: your colour on the "+" buttons, the badges and the chosen category. Prices keep the text colour.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → K · Black & White**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
