1. Open **Team**. Tap **Invite**.
2. Type their e-mail and choose a **Role**.
3. Tap **Send invitation**. The link works for {{invitation_days}} days.

They open the e-mail, make a password and sign in.

![The team page](shot:team)

**Pending invitations** shows who has not joined yet. Tap the cross to cancel one.

**Remove someone:** tap **Remove from team**. They lose access at once.
