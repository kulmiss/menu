**One dish**

1. Open the dish.
2. Change the **Price**. Tap **Save**.

In the app you can also tap **Change a price** on Home.

**Many dishes at once**

1. Open **All dishes**.
2. Tick the dishes. You can also search or filter first.
3. Tap **Change price**.
4. Type a percent, for example 10. For lower prices use a minus: -5.
5. Choose **Round to**, for example to whole numbers.
6. Tap **Apply**.

Sizes change too.

![All dishes with selected dishes](shot:dishes)

**Staff** can change prices only when the owner allowed it (**Team → Can edit prices**).
