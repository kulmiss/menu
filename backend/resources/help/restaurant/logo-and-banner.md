1. Open **Appearance → Logo and banner**.
2. Tap **Logo** and choose your logo. A square picture works best.
3. Tap **Banner** and choose a wide photo. The page shows the best size.
4. Under **Logo and name**, choose **On the banner** or **Below the banner**.
5. Tap **Save**.

The banner is cropped to the shape of your menu style. It is also the picture people see when you share your menu link (see [Social preview](help:social-preview)).

To hide them, turn off **Show the logo** or **Show the banner**.
