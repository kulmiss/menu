Style **L · Colourful** gives each category its own bright colour: its name, its dishes' cards and its prices. The first colour is your brand colour; the others are made from it, so they always go together. Text on every colour stays easy to read.

![Style L · Colourful](shot:style-colourful)

- The category buttons at the top have the same colours, so guests find "Juices" or "Ice cream" fast.
- Buttons move a little when tapped. Phones set to "reduce motion" get no movement.
- **Dishes with a photo** show a rounded photo at the start. Without photos the cards hold the name, the text and the price.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → L · Colourful**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
