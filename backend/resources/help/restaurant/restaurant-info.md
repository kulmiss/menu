Open **Restaurant info**.

**About and contact:** a short description, address, map link, phone, e-mail, social media and website. Leave a field empty to hide it.

**Wi-Fi:** type the network name and password. Turn on **Show Wi-Fi on the menu**.

**Opening hours**

1. For each day, tap **Add hours** and set the times.
2. Closed for lunch? Tap **Add time after a break**.
3. Same hours every weekday? Tap **Copy to Tuesday–Friday**.
4. Tap **Save hours**.

A closing time earlier than the opening time runs past midnight.

**Holidays:** under **Holidays and closed days**, add the dates and a note. Guests see "Closed today" and a notice before.
