Style **J · Somali** has an ocean-blue band at the top with a woven-basket stripe and a wave at its edge. Each category starts with a small aqal (the Somali dome house). The colours are ocean and sky blue, white, sand and warm orange. It doesn't use the flag.

![Style J · Somali](shot:style-somali)

- Somali dish names like **bariis iskukaris**, **hilib ari**, **sambuusa**, **canjeero** and **muufo** fit well, also long ones.
- **Dishes with a photo** show a rounded photo at the start. Without a photo, a small woven mark sits before the name.
- **Your colour** is used for the category names, the prices and the buttons.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → J · Somali**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
