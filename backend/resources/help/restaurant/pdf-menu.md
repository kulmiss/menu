1. Open **Menu**.
2. Tap **Download PDF menu**.
3. Choose the **Menu** (or All menus)<!-- if branches -->, the **Branch**<!-- endif -->, **With photos** or not, and the **Layout** (1 or 2 columns).
4. Tap **Download PDF**.

The PDF uses your colour and style. Hidden<!-- if sold_out --> and sold-out<!-- endif --> dishes are left out.

Guests can download it too, when **Show "Download menu (PDF)" link** is on in **Appearance**.
