In **Appearance → 5. What guests see** you can turn these on or off:

- **Show prices**
- **Show descriptions**
- **Show calories**
<!-- if share -->
- **Show "Share menu" button**
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if pdf_menu -->
- **Show "Download menu (PDF)" link**
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if order_list -->
- **Show "Add to my order" (+) buttons on my menu** (see [My order](help:my-order) and [Turn My order on or off](help:my-order-switch))
<!-- endif -->

Dish photos on or off is the **Text** layout in **2. Layout** (see [Menu layouts](help:menu-layouts)). The logo and banner switches are in **4. Logo & banner**.

Tap **Save**. Guests see it at once.

Opening hours, address and Wi-Fi come from **Restaurant info**. Leave a field empty to hide it.
