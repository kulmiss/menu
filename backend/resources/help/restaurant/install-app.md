The **Menu Admin** app does the daily jobs on your phone: prices, photos, sold out, sharing.

1. Open the **Google Play Store** (Android) or the **App Store** (iPhone).
2. Search for **Menu Admin**.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. Open the app and sign in with the same e-mail and password as the website.

![The app's sign-in screen](appshot:app-sign-in)

The app asks to send notifications, for example when your monthly report is ready. You can change this later in **Settings**.
