Style **G · Street Board** looks like the board above a food counter: dark green, tall capital headings, and each category in its own dashed frame. It stays easy to read, with no hand-drawn letters.

![Style G · Street Board](shot:style-board)

- **Prices are on small tags** in your colour. Bright colours (yellow, orange, light green) look best; the text on the tag is always readable.
- **Dishes with a photo** show it pinned at the side. Without photos, the tag sits at the end of the dish name.
- **Sold out** dishes lose their colour tag and show "Sold out".
- The board is always dark, also on phones in light mode.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → G · Street Board**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
