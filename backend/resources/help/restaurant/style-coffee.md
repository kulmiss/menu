Style **M · Coffee House** has a dark-roast band at the top with a jebena, cups and steam drawn in gold, and a soft coffee-bean pattern. The page is cream, the photos are round and dotted lines sit between the dishes. Each category starts with a small icon in a brown circle.

![Style M · Coffee House](shot:style-coffee)

- **Category icons** follow the category name, in English, Amharic, Somali or Arabic: "Coffee" or "ቡና" gets a cup, "Cakes" a cake, "Breakfast" an egg. A name we don't know gets the next icon of the style.
- **Your own pictures:** upload a **banner** (Appearance) and it replaces the drawing at the top. Add a **photo to a category** (Menu editor → the category → Photo) and it replaces that category's icon.
- **Turn the pictures off:** under the style, switch off **Show the style's pictures and icons**. The colours stay.
- **Dishes with a photo** show a round photo at the start. Without a photo, a small coffee bean sits before the name.
- **Your colour** is used for the prices, the buttons and the "+".

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → M · Coffee House**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
