**Password**

1. Open **Settings → Security**.
2. Type your **Current password** and a new one (at least 10 characters).
3. Tap **Save password**.

Forgot it? On the sign-in page tap **Forgot password?**. We send you a link.

**E-mail:** open **Settings → Profile**, type the new address and your password. We send a link to the new address. Your address changes when you tap it (within 1 hour).

Changing your password in the app signs out your other phones.
