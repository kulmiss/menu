**Add-ons** are choices guests can add, like extra toppings or milk type. They are information only.

1. Open **Add-ons**. Tap **New add-on group**.
2. Type a name, for example "Extra toppings".
3. Add options, each with an **Extra price** if needed.
4. Choose **At least** and **At most**, for example "choose up to 2".
5. Tap **Save**. Then open a dish, tab **Add-ons**, and tick the group.

**Tags** are labels on a dish. Built-in tags (Vegan, Spicy, Halal, Gluten-free, New, Popular) are translated by themselves. Add your own on **Tags**, for example "Chef's pick".

**Allergens:** open a dish, tab **Tags & allergens**, and tick them. Guests see them in the dish details.
