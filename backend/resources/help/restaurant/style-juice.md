Style **T · Juice & Drinks** is fresh and bright: a deep green band with a layered spris glass, a lemon slice and fruit drawn at the top, and avocado, mango and papaya layers along its edge. Dishes are rounded photo cards, two per row; each card gets an avocado, mango or papaya line in turn, with a matching pale price pill.

![Style T · Juice & Drinks](shot:style-juice)

- **Category icons** follow the category name: "Spris", "Smoothies" or "Shakes" get a tall glass, "Fresh" or "Fruit" a citrus slice, "Juice" a glass. A name we don't know gets the next icon of the style.
- **Your own pictures:** a **banner** (Appearance) replaces the drawing at the top. A **category photo** (Menu editor → the category → Photo) replaces that category's icon.
- **Turn the pictures off:** under the style, switch off **Show the style's pictures and icons**.
- It follows the guest's phone: light by day, dark at night.
- **Your colour** is used for the buttons and the chosen category. Pick a fresh colour (green, orange, pink) for the best look.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → T · Juice & Drinks**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
