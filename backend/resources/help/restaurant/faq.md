**1. Is it really free?** Yes. The {{signup_plan}} plan has no end date. Paid plans add more. See [plans](help:plans-compared).

**2. Do guests need an app?** No. They scan the QR code and the menu opens in their browser.

**3. Can guests order and pay online?** No.<!-- if order_list --> "My order" is a list they show the waiter. Nothing is sent or paid.<!-- endif -->

**4. Do I need to print new QR codes when I change the menu?** No. The code always opens your newest menu.

**5. Do I need new QR codes when I change my menu address?** No. Printed codes keep working.

**6. How many dishes can I add?** {{signup_plan.items}} on the {{signup_plan}} plan.

**7. How many photos?** Up to {{photos_per_dish}} per dish. In total {{signup_plan.photos}} on the {{signup_plan}} plan.

**8. Can I hide a dish for a day?** Yes: [hide it](help:hide-a-dish)<!-- if sold_out --> or mark it [sold out](help:sold-out)<!-- endif -->.

**9. Can prices change by themselves?** <!-- if price_periods -->Yes, with [happy hour prices](help:happy-hour).<!-- endif --><!-- if !price_periods -->Not on your plan.<!-- endif -->

**10. Can I have more than one branch?** <!-- if branches -->Yes: see [Add a branch](help:branches).<!-- endif --><!-- if !branches -->Not on your plan. See [plans](help:plans-compared).<!-- endif -->

**11. Can my staff help?** Yes. [Invite them](help:team) as Staff.

**12. Can I see how many people look at my menu?** The dashboard shows today. <!-- if insights -->[Insights](help:insights) shows more.<!-- endif -->

**13. Does the menu work on slow internet?** Yes. It is small and fast. Style D is fastest.

**14. Can I print a paper menu?** <!-- if pdf_menu -->Yes: [PDF menu](help:pdf-menu).<!-- endif --><!-- if !pdf_menu -->Not on your plan.<!-- endif -->

**15. Is my data safe?** Yes. We make backups every day. Turn on [two-factor sign-in](help:two-factor).

**16. Can I use my phone only?** Yes. The website works on phones, and there is [an app](help:install-app).

**17. How do I pay?** By mobile money or bank transfer. See [Upgrade your plan](help:upgrade).

**18. What happens when my plan ends?** See [When your plan ends](help:plan-ended).

**19. Can I change the currency?** Contact us. Prices are not converted.

**20. Can I delete my account?** Yes. Contact us. We delete your restaurant and data.
