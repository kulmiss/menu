A **price period** changes prices at set times, for example every day from 17:00 to 19:00. Guests see the normal price crossed out and the special price.

1. Open **Promotions → Price periods**. Tap **New price period**.
2. Type the **Name shown to guests**, for example "Happy hour".
3. Choose the **Days** and the times **From** and **Until**.
4. Tap **Add a dish**. For each dish choose **% off** or **New price**.
5. Turn on **Use this price period**. Tap **Save**.

**What guests see now** shows if a period is running. The menu changes by itself when a period starts or ends. Times are in your restaurant's time zone. An end time earlier than the start runs past midnight.
