title: The app doesn't work
topic: problems
summary: Update, maintenance, or a sign-in problem.
order: 50
public: true
keywords: app crash, app not loading, update, maintenance
---

1. **Update the app** in the Play Store or App Store.
2. **"Down for maintenance"?** Wait a few minutes and tap **Try again**.
3. **Signed out?** Sign in again. A phone not used for 60 days is signed out by itself.
4. **No internet?** See [Using the app offline](help:app-offline).
5. Still not working? Use the website at {{admin_url}} and contact us.
