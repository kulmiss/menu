1. **Grace time:** for {{grace_days}} days you can still edit everything. A banner asks you to renew.
2. **Read-only:** for {{read_only_days}} more days, guests still see your menu, but you can't change it.
3. **Offline:** then your menu goes offline until you renew.

Your data is not deleted. Renew any time: see [Upgrade your plan](help:upgrade).
