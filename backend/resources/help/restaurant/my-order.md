With **My order**, guests tap **Add to my order** on dishes. They see the total at the bottom. Then they show the list to the waiter.

It is **not** an online order. Nothing is sent to you. The list says: "This is not an order. Show it to your waiter."

![My order on a guest's phone](shot:guest-my-order)

**Turn it on or off:** open **Appearance** and use **Show "My order" list**.

**Numbers:** the dashboard<!-- if insights --> and Insights<!-- endif --> show how many lists guests made, the average value, and the most added dishes. They are counted without names. In the app, open the **Orders** tab.
