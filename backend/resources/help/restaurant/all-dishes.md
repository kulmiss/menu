**All dishes** shows every dish of every menu in one list.

- **Search** by name.
- **Filter** by menu, category or status: On the menu, Hidden, No photo<!-- if sold_out -->, Sold out<!-- endif -->.
- **Sort** by name, price or last change.

Tick dishes to change many at once: **Hide**, **Show**, **Change price**, **Delete**<!-- if sold_out -->, **Sold out**, **Available**<!-- endif -->.

Deleting removes the dishes and their photos from every menu. It can't be undone.
