Style **E · Mesob** looks like a family table: warm cream colours, woven bands at the top and around each category, and round photos like plates on a mesob.

![Style E · Mesob](shot:style-mesob)

- **Your colour** is used in the woven bands, the prices and the buttons. Dark, warm colours (red, brown, green) look best.
- **Dishes with a photo** show it in a round frame. **Dishes without a photo** get a small diamond, so the list still looks tidy. The style works well with no photos at all.
- **Amharic, Somali and English names** all fit. You can write both, for example "Doro wot · ዶሮ ወጥ".
- On phones set to **dark mode**, the menu turns dark brown with the same bands.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → E · Mesob**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
