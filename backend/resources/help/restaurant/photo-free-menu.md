Two ways:

<!-- if style_minimal -->
1. Choose style **D · Minimal**. It shows names, short lines and prices, with no dish photos. It opens fastest, also on slow internet.
<!-- endif -->
2. In any style, turn off **Show photos** in **Appearance**. Your photos stay saved. Turn it on again to show them.

You can also hide the photos of one dish: open the dish and turn off **Show photo on menu**.
