Open **Insights**. Choose the **Period** (last 7, 30 or 90 days)<!-- if branches --> and the **Branch**<!-- endif -->.

- **Menu views**: how many times your menu page was opened.
- **QR scans**: views that came from a QR code.
- **Dish details opened**: how often guests opened a dish.
- **Most viewed dishes**: your most popular dishes.
- **Scans per QR code**: which tables and posters bring guests in.

Search engines, link previews and speed tests are not counted. We count without cookies and without storing anyone's address.

![Insights](shot:insights)

**Export:** tap **Export** for a PDF report or CSV files.

**Monthly report:** turn on **E-mail me the monthly report**. Owners get last month's report as a PDF on the 1st.
