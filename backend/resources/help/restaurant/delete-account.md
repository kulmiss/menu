Only the **Owner** of a restaurant can ask us to delete it.

1. Write to us from the e-mail address of the Owner's account: [Contact us](help:contact-support).
2. Tell us your restaurant name and menu address, and that you want it deleted.
3. We check that the request comes from you, then delete the restaurant: the menu, photos, QR codes, team and statistics.

Good to know:

- **Your menu stops working** for guests at once. Printed QR codes will show "not found".
- **A deleted restaurant is kept for 30 days**, so it can be restored if you change your mind. Then it is deleted for good.
- **Invoices and payment records** are kept as long as accounting rules require.
- **Backups:** deleted data can stay in a backup until that backup is removed.
