Style **I · Islamic** has a star pattern at the top, an arch around your name, a small lantern and arched photo cards, two per row. The colours are calm: deep teal, cream and soft gold. It has no pictures of people or animals and no religious text.

![Style I · Islamic](shot:style-islamic)

Under the style you find two options. Both are off until you turn them on:

- **Show a "Halal" badge**: a small badge under your restaurant's name. Turn it on only if all your food is halal.
- **Ramadan mode**: the top turns into a night sky with a crescent. Turn it off again after Ramadan.

Dishes without a photo show a small gold star instead. If your menu has no photos at all, each dish gets a full-width card.

To use it: **Appearance → Menu style → I · Islamic**, then **Save**. See all styles in [Menu styles](help:menu-styles).
