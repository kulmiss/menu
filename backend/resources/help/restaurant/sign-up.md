You can make a menu for free. You don't need a card.

1. Open **{{site_url}}/auth/sign-up**.
2. Type your name, your **Restaurant name**, your e-mail and a password. Use at least 10 characters. Tap **Show** to check the password.
3. Tick the box for the Terms and the Privacy Policy.
4. Tap **Create my free menu**.
5. Open the e-mail we send you and tap the link. The link works for 24 hours.

Your **menu address** is made from the restaurant name (the page shows it, e.g. {{site_url}}/buna-bread). Your prices are shown in the platform's currency. Need another address or currency? [Contact us](help:contact-support).

![The sign-up page](shot:signup)

You start on the **{{signup_plan}}** plan. See [what each plan includes](help:plans-compared).

**No e-mail?** Look in your spam folder. On the "Check your inbox" page you can tap **Resend email** or **Change it** if you typed the wrong address.

**"Your account is waiting for approval"?** Sometimes our team checks new restaurants first. You can set up your menu now. Guests can see it once it is approved.
