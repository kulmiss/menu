**Announcements** show on restaurant dashboards (and as a push in the app).

1. Open **Announcements**. Write a **Title** and **Text**.
2. Choose the **Type**: Info or Warning.
3. Choose **Who sees it**: every restaurant, or some plans or restaurants.
4. Set **Show from** and **Until**. Tap **Publish**.

**Messages** are e-mails to restaurant owners: news, offers and reminders.

1. Open **Messages → New email**.
2. Write the **Subject** and **Message**. Placeholders like the restaurant name are filled in for each owner.
3. Choose the **Recipients**. Check the preview and **send yourself a test** first.
4. Send. They go out a few per minute (**E-mails per minute**, keep it below your mail provider's limit). Owners who switched off news don't get them.

For the mobile app only, use [Mobile app: send a push notification](help:mobile-push).
