Open **Settings → General**. Scroll to **Terms of use**, **Privacy policy**, **About us page**, **Contact us page** and **Cookie info page**.

The defaults are a **draft**. Read them and change them for your business and your country. Ask a lawyer.

The texts are shown at `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/about`, `/contact` and `/cookies`. Terms and Privacy are also shown at sign-up and in the app; every saved version of them is kept, so you can show what was valid on a date. The Contact us page also shows the WhatsApp, phone and e-mail from **Footer**.

**View page** opens the page. **Reset to default text** puts the standard text back.
