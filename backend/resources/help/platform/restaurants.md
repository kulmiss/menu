**Restaurants** lists every restaurant. Search by name or address. Filter by **State**: trial, active, grace, read-only, unavailable, suspended, pending.

**Create one:** tap **New restaurant**. Type the name, menu address, owner's name and e-mail, trial plan and days. The owner gets an e-mail to choose a password.

**Open one** to see:

- **Subscription**: plan, trial until, paid until. **Payments** records a payment (see [Payments](help:payments)).
- **Features**: turn single features on or off for this restaurant, or leave **As the plan**.
- **Team**: the people and their roles.
- **Log in as owner**: see [Log in as](help:log-in-as).
- **E-mail a password link**: for an owner who can't sign in.
- **Suspend**: the menu shows "unavailable" and the team can't make changes. Type a reason; the owner sees it.
- **Delete restaurant**: it can be restored for 30 days, then it is removed for good.

**New sign-ups waiting for approval** have **Approve** and **Reject** buttons. A rejected owner gets your reason by e-mail.
