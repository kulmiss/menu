**Settings → Landing page** is the public home page at {{site_url}}.

- **Headline** and **subline**: leave empty for the default words.
- **Sections**: turn parts on or off (how it works, features, styles, speed, pricing, testimonials, FAQ).
- **Testimonials**: only real quotes from real restaurants, with their permission. The section is hidden while it is empty.
- **Social links** in the footer.

Prices on the landing page come from **Plans**: you never type them here.

**Settings → Logo & link preview**

- **Logo**: shown at the top of e-mails and invoices. No logo = the platform name in text.
- **Link preview**: the picture apps show when a restaurant without a banner shares its menu link (1200 × 630).

**Settings → Billing**: your company name, address and tax number on invoices, the invoice number prefix, the footer text and the VAT rate.
