---

- **"I can't sign in."** Open the restaurant → **E-mail a password link**. Lost 2FA phone and codes? You can't turn it off for them; ask them to use a recovery code, or make a new owner and remove the old one.
- **"My menu is offline."** Check **Subscription**: paid until, grace, read-only. Record the payment.
- **"I need a feature."** Check the plan and the restaurant's **Features**. Upgrade the plan, or turn it on for this restaurant.
- **"Something looks wrong."** Use [Log in as](help:log-in-as) to see what they see.
- Point them to their own **Help** page: every screen has a **?** button.
