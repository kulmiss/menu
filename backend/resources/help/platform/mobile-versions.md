Open **Mobile app → Versions**.

- **Latest version**: the newest version in the stores.
- **Recommended update below**: older apps see "A new version of the app is ready" once. People can close it.
- **Minimum supported version**: older apps **must** update. They see "Update required" and can't do anything else.
- **Release notes**: shown in both messages.

Versions look like `1.4.0`. The minimum can't be higher than the latest.

**Order:** publish the new version in the stores first. Wait until the store shows it. Then raise the minimum.
