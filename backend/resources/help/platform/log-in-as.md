---
title: "Log in as" a restaurant owner
topic: support
summary: See and fix a restaurant exactly as the owner sees it.
order: 10
pages: /platform/restaurants
---

1. Open **Restaurants** and the restaurant.
2. Tap **Log in as owner**.

One click, no reason needed. You see **Viewing as …** at the top. Tap **Back to platform** to stop.

- It ends by itself after **2 hours**.
- It is **not recorded**: no log line, and changes count as the owner's. Use it with care.
- Some things stay blocked: the owner's password, e-mail, two-factor, team removal, closing the account.

It works in the app too: open the restaurant and tap **Log in as**.
