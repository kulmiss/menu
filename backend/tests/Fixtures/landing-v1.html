Questions and answers

Short answers to what owners ask most.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit. You never need a card.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They scan the QR code with the phone camera. Your menu opens at once.

Can I change prices myself?

Yes. Sign in, change the price and save. Every phone shows the new price.

I have many branches. Does it work?

Yes. Each branch gets its own link and QR codes. See the plans for how many.

Can I print my menu?

Yes. Print QR posters, table cards and stickers. Some plans also make a PDF menu.

Can I add photos of my dishes?

Yes. Add photos from your phone. We make them small, so your menu opens quickly.

How do I upgrade?

Message us. Pay by mobile money or bank transfer. We switch your plan for you.

How do I get help?

Message us any time. A real person will help you.
