# Mobile app: send a push notification

*Write a message to all app users, or some of them.*

1. Open **Mobile app → Push**.
2. Type the **Title** and the **Message**.
3. Add what it **Opens** (optional): an app screen like `/inbox`, or an `https://` link.
4. Choose **Who**: Everyone, Restaurant teams (by role, plan or restaurant numbers) or Super Admins.
5. Tap **Review and send**. You see how many people get it, and how many get it on their phone.
6. Tap **Send now**.

It goes to the in-app inbox of everyone chosen who is signed in to the app, and as a push to phones that allow it. **Sent pushes** shows the history.

Up to 10 a minute. Don't send too many: people turn notifications off.
