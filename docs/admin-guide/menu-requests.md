# Menu requests ("Add my menu for me")

*Restaurants send photos of their paper menu; you type it in.*

Restaurants can tap **Add my menu for me** on their Dashboard and send photos (or a PDF) of their paper menu. Every Super Admin gets an e-mail.

1. Open **Menu requests**. New ones are at the top, with the photos and the owner's note. Tap a photo to see it full size.
2. Tap **Start**, so the owner sees "We are typing in your menu now".
3. **Open restaurant → Log in as**, and type the menu in (Menu editor, or Import for a spreadsheet).
4. Back here, tap **Mark as done**. The owner gets an e-mail "Your menu is ready".

The photos are private (only Super Admins can open them). Done requests and their photos are deleted after 30 days; **Delete photos** removes them at once. A restaurant can have 3 open requests at a time.

Switch it off in **Settings → Sign-up → "Add my menu for me"**.
