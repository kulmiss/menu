# Website footer

*The footer of the website: columns of links, contact, social links, the app badge, history.*

**Footer** (top menu) is the bottom of every website page: the home page, sign-up and sign-in, Help, Terms, Privacy and the other public pages. Restaurant menus never show it.

**Parts**: switch the picture band, **Contact**, the **Google Play badge**, the **Trust line** and the **Back to top** button on or off.

**Columns and links**

- Change a column's title, switch it off, or remove it. **Add a column** (up to 6).
- Each link has a text and **Goes to**: a page of this site from the list, or **A web address…** (`https://`, `mailto:` or `tel:`). Other kinds of link are refused.
- Change the order: drag the handle, or use the arrows. Up to 12 links a column.
- On phones the columns fold open and shut; the contact block always shows.

**Contact**: empty fields are hidden. The WhatsApp number becomes a green chat button.

**Social links**: only the networks with an address show. Search engines get them too.

**Google Play badge**: shows only when the Google Play address is set (`https://play.google.com/…`).

**Trust line**: write only what is true, for example "Free plan forever".

**Draft, preview and Publish**

1. Change what you need and click **Save draft**. Guests still see the live footer.
2. Check the **Preview**: **Phone** or **Computer**, **Light** or **Dark**.
3. Click **Publish**. The new footer is live at once.

**Discard draft** throws your unpublished changes away.

**Version history** keeps every published footer. **Restore** puts an earlier one live again in one click; it becomes a new version, so nothing is lost. Every change is in **Activity**.
