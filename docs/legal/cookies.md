## In short

- **Menu pages and the public pages of this website set no cookies.** No advertising cookies, no analytics, no trackers.
- **The sign-in and sign-up pages, and the admin,** use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and safe.

## Cookies when you sign in or sign up

When you open the sign-in or sign-up page, and while a restaurant team member or a Super Admin is signed in, the browser keeps:

- **a session cookie**, so you stay signed in while you work. It is removed when you sign out or close the browser after a while;
- **a security cookie (XSRF-TOKEN)**, which stops other websites from sending forms in your name;
- **a "remember me" cookie**, only if you tick "Remember me" on the sign-in page.

None of them is used for advertising or shared with others.

## The spam check on sign-up

If the platform has the spam check on, the sign-up page loads Cloudflare Turnstile. Cloudflare may use what it needs for that check. See Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

## "My order" and the saved menu

"My order" and the saved copy of a menu stay in your phone's browser storage, not in cookies. We never receive them. Clearing the browser's data for the site removes them.
