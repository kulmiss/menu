Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Menu collects, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. Menu is run by Kulmiss ("we", "us"). We follow Ethiopian law, including the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

In short:

- Guests don't need an account. Menu pages set no cookies, show no ads and use no trackers, and we don't store guests' IP addresses.
- For restaurants, we keep the account and menu data we need to run Menu.
- We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Restaurant users:** owners, managers and staff with a Menu account.
- **Guests:** people who open a restaurant's menu, and visitors of our website.

Each restaurant decides what it puts on its menu. For questions about a restaurant's food or service, please contact the restaurant.

## 2. What we collect from restaurants

- **Account:** your name, e-mail address, password and language. We store the password only as a secure hash, never as plain text. If you turn on two-step sign-in, its secret is stored encrypted.
- **Team:** the e-mail addresses of people you invite, and their roles.
- **Restaurant information:** name, menu address, branches, address, phone, WhatsApp, e-mail, social links, opening hours, logo, banner and anything else you add.
- **Menu content:** menus, dishes, prices and photos. When you upload a photo, we remove hidden data such as location (EXIF).
- **Plan and billing:** your plan, paid-until date, the payments we record (amount, method, reference) and invoices with the billing details you give us.
- **Settings:** for example, whether you get news e-mails or the monthly report.
- **Sign-in sessions:** while you are signed in, your session is stored with your IP address and browser type. It is removed after it expires.
- **Activity log:** a record of changes in your restaurant (who changed what, and when). It stores a scrambled one-way code of the IP address, not the address itself.
- **Security:** to stop abuse, we use your IP address for a short time (up to one hour) to limit repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts.
- **Support:** e-mails you send us, and notes our team keeps about your account, such as support history.

## 3. What we collect from guests

- Guests don't create an account. We don't ask for a name, e-mail or phone number.
- Menu pages and our website set no cookies, show no ads and use no third-party analytics or trackers.
- **Anonymous statistics:** when a menu is opened, a QR code is scanned, a dish is opened or a dish is added to "My order", we add one to a daily counter for that restaurant. When a guest shows "My order", we add its total to a daily sum. We don't store IP addresses, device IDs or anything else that identifies a person. We look at the browser type only to skip search-engine robots, and we don't store it.
- **"My order":** the list stays on the guest's own phone, in the browser's storage. We never receive the list itself.
- **Saved copy:** the phone keeps a copy of the menu so that it opens fast and works with a weak connection. Clearing the browser's data for the site removes it, together with "My order".
- **Server logs:** like most websites, our hosting provider's web servers may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, page) for a short time, for security and to fix problems.

## 4. Why we use data

- To run Menu: create accounts, show menus, send the e-mails the service needs, record payments and issue invoices.
- To keep Menu safe: secure sign-in, stopping spam and abuse, and making backups.
- To show restaurants their statistics (Insights).
- To help you when you ask for support.
- To send owners news about Menu, unless they have unsubscribed.
- To meet legal duties, such as keeping invoices.

## 5. E-mails we send

- **Account e-mails:** e-mail confirmation, welcome, team invitations, password reset, sign-up approval or rejection, invoices, reminders before your paid time ends, and important service messages. These are needed for your account.
- **Monthly Insights report:** only if you switch it on in Insights.
- **News:** occasional e-mails to owners about new features and offers. Every news e-mail has an unsubscribe link, and you can also switch it off in Settings → Profile. Account e-mails are still sent.

## 6. Who helps us

We use a few service providers. They handle data for us only to run Menu:

- **Hosting:** the servers and database where Menu and all its data are stored.
- **E-mail delivery:** the e-mail service that sends our e-mails.
- **Only if we switch them on:** Cloudflare, for network protection and speed and for the "are you human?" check on the sign-up form; and an off-site storage provider for copies of our backups.

Some of these providers may handle data outside Ethiopia. We share data with others only when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our rights.

Links on a menu, such as WhatsApp, maps or social media, open other services. Their own privacy policies apply there.

## 7. Support access

Our support team can open a restaurant's admin as its owner, to help. They then see what the owner sees. Only our platform administrators can do this, they must use two-step sign-in, and each visit ends automatically after 2 hours.

## 8. How long we keep data

- **Accounts and restaurants:** as long as the account exists.
- **Deleted restaurants:** 30 days, so they can be restored, then deleted for good.
- **Sign-ups that are never confirmed:** deleted after 7 days.
- **Refused sign-ups:** deleted when we refuse them, after we tell you why.
- **Activity log:** as long as the restaurant exists.
- **Guest statistics:** daily counters are deleted after about 13 months.
- **Backups:** we make a backup of the database and photos every day and keep one for each of the last 7 days, 4 weeks and 3 months. Deleted data can stay in a backup until that backup is removed.
- **Invoices and payment records:** as long as accounting rules require, also after an account is deleted.

## 9. Security

- Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
- Two-step sign-in is available to every user and required for our platform administrators.
- Each restaurant can see only its own data, and team roles limit what each person can do.
- Backups are stored in a private folder on the server, not on the public website.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you without delay.

## 10. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- show you the personal data we have about you;
- correct it (you can also change most of it yourself in the app);
- give you a copy of it (owners can also export their menu and statistics in the app, if their plan includes it);
- delete it (we delete accounts and restaurants as described in section 8);
- stop sending you news e-mails.

E-mail info@kulmiss.com from your account's e-mail address. We may need to check that it is really you, and we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Ethiopian Communications Authority.

Guests: we don't keep data that identifies you. To remove "My order" and the saved menu, clear your browser's data for the site.

## 11. Children

Menu accounts are only for adults (18 and over). We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the "Last updated" date. For important changes, we tell owners by e-mail or in the app.
